Made a financial mistake? Call Don T. Overpay.

Two films for Wise's *Don't Overpay* campaign. The premise treats sending money abroad through a traditional bank as the kind of error you'd hire a specialist to fix — so the campaign gives you one, in a green suit, working out of a wood-panelled office lined with currency symbols.

The point underneath the joke is Wise's actual pitch: transparent fees, the mid-market exchange rate, 40+ currencies in one app, and payments that arrive instantly 70% of the time. Be smart. Get Wise.
