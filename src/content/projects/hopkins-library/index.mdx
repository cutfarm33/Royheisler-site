A short series made for Johns Hopkins' archive of the Brody Reading Room renovation. I photographed the space at three points across one day — an hour after open, at peak use around 2 p.m., and twenty minutes after the last student left — to show how the room's proportions read differently against the same light depending on who and what is in it.

Shot on the GFX 100 II with two lenses — a 23mm for the full-room compositions and a 45mm for everything with a person in it. Natural light only, tripod-locked for the open and empty frames, handheld for peak.
