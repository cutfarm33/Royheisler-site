Shot on location across the Tetons over six days. The brief was "warm garments in cold places, don't fake it."

We moved every day — base camp in Jackson, then trucking the crew up by 4:30 a.m. to catch first light at altitude. The camera crew shrank to three by day three when one of the ACs came down with altitude sickness, and we covered the last two shoot days with a simpler rig: Venice 2 on a Ronin, Signature Primes at T2, handheld when the pace required it and sticks when the wind didn't.

The final spot runs 2:18. What isn't in the cut: the morning on day four when the clouds dropped to eye level and we shot the entire call sheet out of order because the weather window was 40 minutes wide and the wardrobe change had to happen in a parked truck.

Roy's role was DP only — shot it for Mira Okafor, who directed.
