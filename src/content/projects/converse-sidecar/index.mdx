A quick turn with Ben Liu for the Chuck 70 relaunch. One afternoon, three blocks in Astoria, a cast of six friends of the director. The whole spot was designed to feel handheld but controlled — we ran the 35 on an Easyrig with a Ronin 2 in the car for the sidecar shots and nothing else.

The brief specified no product shots longer than half a second and no lingering on logos. The edit honors that; the last frame you see the shoes is frame 1386 and they stay on screen for 11 frames.
