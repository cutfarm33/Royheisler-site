MealMap started as a problem I had on the road: I'd eat somewhere good and by the time I tried to remember the name three cities later, I couldn't. Notes apps are a bad fit for it — not structured, not quickly searchable, not visual.

The app is four screens. A map view of everywhere you've eaten, a list view that's actually a filter on the map, an entry detail screen with a single photo and three fields (name, notes, company), and a capture flow that defaults to the current location and camera. No social, no accounts, no AI.

Shipped to TestFlight on 2025-09-02 with 18 friends testing, full public release is intentionally TBD until I've eaten with it for a year.
