An overview film on primary health care for Jhpiego, a Johns Hopkins University affiliate whose mission is to save lives by improving access to critical health care where it's needed most.

Founded in 1973 and headquartered in Baltimore, Jhpiego works in more than 30 countries on maternal, newborn and child health, infectious disease, reproductive health, immunization and women's cancers — building the capacity of health workers through hands-on, skills-based training, and developing low-cost, high-impact solutions alongside governments and communities.
