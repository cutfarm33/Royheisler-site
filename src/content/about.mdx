I'm an award-winning cinematographer, editor, designer, and programmer, and I've been working in the industry for over twenty years. I've worked in some of the most dangerous locations on earth.

In 2005 I was the co-creator, camera operator, and motion graphics specialist for a ten-episode series on A&E titled *Random 1*. The series aired from 2005 to 2006. *Random 1* just signed a new contract with The Oprah Network.

I was the head cinematographer for The Mountain Gorilla Veterinary Project for seven years, an organization responsible for the protection and revitalization of the endangered mountain gorillas of Uganda, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of Congo. I work directly with wild gorillas and with the amazing doctors who are saving them — [some of that work is here](/work/gorilla-doctors).

I've been a photographer and cinematographer at Paris and New York fashion week for Valentino and Saks Jandel, and I also shoot national commercials, music videos, and short films. I'm also a certified drone operator, and I've shot aerial footage all over the world.

My camera credits include work for Intel, Volkswagen, Amazon, Smithsonian, Hewlett Packard, Reuters, ESPN, BBC, Travel Channel, A&E, Johns Hopkins, and many others. If you want to see more, [the work index](/work) has the full set.
